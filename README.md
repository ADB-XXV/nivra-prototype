
#NIVRA- The One Day Gig 🚀
**The Hyper-Local Student Micro-Gig Marketplace**

NIVRA is a responsive web application designed to bridge the gap between college students seeking flexible, short-term work and local employers needing immediate assistance. 

## ✨ Key Features
* **Student Profiles & Verification:** Secure sign-up, skills tagging, and availability calendars.
* **Employer Dashboard:** Business registration, active gig management, and applicant tracking.
* **Gig Creation & Management:** Post short-term gigs with required skills, pay rates, and locations.
* **Smart Discovery Feed:** Hyper-local job feed with advanced filtering.
* **Matching Engine:** One-click applications and intuitive candidate selection.
* **Instant Payout Interface:** Digital payment processing and transaction tracking.
* **Two-Way Rating System:** Rate reliability and workplace safety.
